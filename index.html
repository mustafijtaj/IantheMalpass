The Book

A call back to the Great Commission

You were called to be a disciple: praying, obeying, watching, evangelising, and walking in the power of the Holy Spirit every single day.

Yet somewhere along the way, the Church traded radical discipleship for comfortable religion. The Mandate confronts the passive, challenges the comfortable, and equips every believer, regardless of age or stage, to live the full, purpose-filled life they were created for: from the secret place to the mission field, from renewing the mind to walking in the Spirit, from loving your neighbour to shaking nations.

The mandate is clear and the Spirit is ready. The question is, are you?

    About the Author

    Hephzibah Malpass

    “I wrote it for anyone who has ever wondered where they fit into it.”

    Hephzibah writes to help ordinary believers find their place in the commission Jesus gave His followers, and to live it out in the everyday places where God has put them. The Mandate and its companion journal are her invitation to a faith that is read, prayed and practised.

    If you read it, she would love to hear what you think, and to have you share it with someone who would be blessed by it.
